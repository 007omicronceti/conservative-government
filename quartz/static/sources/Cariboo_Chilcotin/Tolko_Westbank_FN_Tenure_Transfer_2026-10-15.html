Westbank First Nation and Tolko Industries Ltd. Announce Landmark Forestry Partnership and Tenure Transfer Agreement

by NTITYIXOctober 15, 2026

October 14, 2026 – For Immediate Release

Ntityix Resources Limited Partnership, the forestry and natural resource enterprise wholly owned by Westbank First Nation via Ntityix Development LP, and Tolko Industries Ltd. today announced the completion of transformative forestry agreements that will transfer Forest Licence A98912 to Ntityix while establishing a long-term fibre supply partnership between the organizations.

Ntityix Resources Limited Partnership & Tolko Industries Limited

The agreements represent a significant step forward in advancing Indigenous participation and leadership in British Columbia’s forest sector while maintaining a stable, long-term fibre supply relationship with Tolko’s manufacturing operations. Under the agreements, Ntityix will assume ownership and management of Forest Licence A98912, with an allowable annual cut of 52,100 cubic metres, and will become responsible for stewardship, operational planning, forest management, and licence administration.

The licence transfer is accompanied by a 15-year Fibre Supply Agreement, automatically renewable under specified conditions, under which Ntityix will supply timber from Forest Licence A98912 as well as additional volumes from other Westbank First Nation forestry tenures to Tolko.

“This agreement reflects a shared commitment to reconciliation, economic development, and responsible forest stewardship,” said Mic Werstuik, CEO of Ntityix Development Corporation. “The transfer of this tenure expands Westbank First Nation’s participation in the forest economy and creates a platform for long-term Indigenous leadership in sustainable forestry management while supporting employment, capacity development, and stewardship within our traditional territory.”

Ntityix Resources Limited Partnership & Tolko Industries Limited

As part of the transaction, Ntityix will take responsibility for the management of the transferred licence and associated forestry operations. The agreements also contemplate provincial capacity funding to support the development of forestry management expertise, stewardship programs, operational planning, and licence administration capabilities within Ntityix.

“Building strong partnerships with Indigenous communities is fundamental to the future of British Columbia’s forest sector,” said Pino Pucci, President & CEO, Tolko. “This agreement creates long-term certainty for both organizations while supporting Indigenous forestry ownership, economic participation, and sustainable forest management.”

Under the Fibre Supply Agreement, Ntityix will manage and harvest timber from multiple forestry tenures, including Forest Licence A98912, First Nations Woodland Licence N2B, and Community Forest Agreement K1P. Combined, these forest tenures represent an allowable annual harvest volume exceeding 115,000 cubic metres and provide the foundation for a long-term forestry business led by Westbank First Nation.

Ntityix Resources Limited Partnership & Tolko Industries Limited

“This is good for Westbank First Nation’s corporate group because it helps us create certainty during uncertain times while expanding the tenure base for long-term economic growth,” said Chief Robert Louie. “The deal creates lasting contracting opportunities for our membership. These agreements represent a win-win for both Ntityix and Tolko because we are exchanging management and economic rights of the timber while strengthening the partnership with one of our biggest customers.”

The partnership also establishes a collaborative framework for annual operating plans, operational coordination, cost benchmarking, and continuous improvement initiatives designed to optimize forest management, harvesting efficiencies, and fibre utilization. Both organizations have committed to working together to maximize economic, environmental, and community benefits arising from the forest tenures.

Ntityix Resources Limited Partnership & Tolko Industries Limited

The agreements remain subject to applicable regulatory approvals and conditions under the British Columbia Forest Act and related provincial processes.

About Ntityix Resources Limited Partnership

Ntityix Resources Limited Partnership is a wholly owned limited partnership of Westbank First Nation. Ntityix manages and develops natural resource, forestry, and related economic development opportunities on behalf of Westbank First Nation, creating sustainable economic benefits while advancing land stewardship and community priorities.

About Westbank First Nation

Westbank First Nation is a self-governing First Nation located in the Okanagan region of British Columbia. Through its economic development entities, Westbank First Nation pursues opportunities that support community prosperity, self-determination, environmental stewardship, and long-term sustainability.

About Tolko Industries Ltd.

Tolko Industries Ltd. is a diversified forest products company with manufacturing facilities and forest operations across Western Canada and the U.S. South. Tolko produces lumber, plywood, veneer, oriented strand board, and other wood products for domestic and international markets.
