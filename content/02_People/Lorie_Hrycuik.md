# Lorie Hrycuik

## Current Role
- **Title:** Deputy Minister, Emergency Management BC (EMBC)
- **Ministry:** [[01_Positions/16_Public_Safety_and_Solicitor_General]]
- **Replaces:** Teresa Dobmeier (former DM, Emergency Management and Climate Readiness)

## Career & Background Summary
- **Ministry of Labour (June 2023–Oct 2026):** Served as Deputy Minister of Labour (initially Acting DM in June 2023, confirmed DM through October 2026) prior to Labour's merger with Post-Secondary Education.
- **Ministry of Health (2019):** Served as **Assistant Deputy Minister / Executive Lead, Population and Public Health Division**, testifying before the Legislative Assembly's Public Accounts Committee on December 17, 2019 alongside Health DM Stephen Brown and Provincial Health Officer Dr. Bonnie Henry.

## OSINT Watchpoints
- **Crisis-Response Background:** Moves from Labour into Emergency Management BC (now folded back into Public Safety), bringing prior public-health emergency and operational action-plan experience from the Ministry of Health.
