# Ministry of Public Safety and Solicitor General

## Assigned Senior Public Servants & Political Leadership
- **Deputy Minister (Public Safety & Solicitor General):** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Tara_Richards]]
- **Deputy Minister, Emergency Management BC (EMBC):** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Lorie_Hrycuik]]
- **Cabinet Minister:** Surrey-Serpentine River MLA (per Nov 20, 2026 Victoria News swearing-in report)

## Structural Mandate
Oversees policing, corrections, coroners service, road safety, victim services, and re-absorbs **Emergency Management BC (EMBC)** under a dedicated Deputy Minister (consolidating the former standalone *Ministry of Emergency Management and Climate Readiness*).
