# Ministry of Children and Family Development

## Assigned Senior Public Servant
- **Deputy Minister:** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Kaye_Krishna]]

## Structural Mandate
Administers child protection, Indigenous child and family development transitions, adoption/foster/kinship care, child and youth mental health, and support services for children and youth with support needs.
