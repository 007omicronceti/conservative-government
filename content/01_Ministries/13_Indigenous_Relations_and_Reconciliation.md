# Ministry of Indigenous Relations and Reconciliation

## Leadership Roster (Effective Nov 20, 2026)
- **Minister:** [[02_Cabinet/Scott_McInnis]]
- **Deputy Minister:** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Alex_MacLennan]]

## What This Ministry Controls
- **Negotiations & Agreements:** Treaty negotiations, reconciliation agreements, and revenue-sharing partnerships with First Nations.
- **DRIPA & Land-Use Frameworks:** Provincial policy on DRIPA, consultation protocols, and shared decision-making.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **DRIPA & Aboriginal Title:** Central to any legislative changes to DRIPA and land-use certainty across Tŝilhqot'in and Secwépemc territories—see [[04_Dossiers/Cariboo_Chilcotin/Dossier_DRIPA_Repeal_Cariboo_Title]].
- **Economic Partnerships:** Shapes First Nations benefit-sharing agreements affecting regional timber and mining projects.
