# Ministry of Environment

## Leadership Roster (Effective Nov 20, 2026)
- **Minister:** [[02_Cabinet/Aaliya_Warbus]] (Áʼa:líya Warbus)
- **Deputy Minister & Chief Executive Assessment Officer:** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Lori_Halls]]

## What Changed in Doerkson's Restructuring
Streamlined to the **Ministry of Environment**, pairing Minister Áʼa:líya Warbus with Deputy Minister [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Lori_Halls]], who also serves as **Chief Executive Assessment Officer** overseeing the Environmental Assessment Office (EAO).

## What This Ministry Controls
- **Environmental Assessments (EAO):** Reviews, sets conditions for, and recommends approval or rejection of major mining and infrastructure projects.
- **Environmental Protection:** Waste discharge permits, water quality standards, contaminated sites, and conservation officer enforcement.
- **BC Parks:** Provincial parks and protected areas.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **Mining Gatekeeper:** The EAO is the key regulatory gate for [[04_Dossiers/Cariboo_Chilcotin/Dossier_Yellowhead_Copper_100Mile]] and [[04_Dossiers/Cariboo_Chilcotin/Dossier_Cariboo_Gold_Project]].
- **Watershed & Wildfire Recovery:** Oversees environmental compliance and recovery after regional wildfires and landslides.
