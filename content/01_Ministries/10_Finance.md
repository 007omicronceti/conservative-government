# Ministry of Finance

## Leadership Roster (Effective Nov 20, 2026)
- **Minister:** [[02_Cabinet/Peter_Milobar]]
- **Deputy Minister:** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Heather_Wood]]
- **Associate Deputy Ministers:** Jordan Goss; Alex Chandler (Acting); Erin McEwan (Acting, LDB)
- **Agency Heads:** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Debbie_Godfrey]] (BC PSA); [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Steve_Vanagas]] (GCPE); [[03_Deputy_Ministers/John_Davison]] (PSEC)

## What This Ministry Controls
- **Budget & Treasury Board:** Ministry spending envelopes, debt management, and capital project approvals.
- **Tax Policy & Revenue:** Provincial income tax, PST, property transfer tax, and resource royalties.
- **Public Sector Bargaining & Comms:** Houses PSEC, BC Public Service Agency, and GCPE.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **Spring 2027 Budget:** Controls capital funding for Cariboo Memorial Hospital, rural ER staffing, and Highway 97 upgrades.
- **Public Sector Bargaining:** PSEC mandates directly affect healthcare, education, and wildfire staffing negotiations.
