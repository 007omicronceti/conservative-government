# Ministry of Agriculture

## Assigned Senior Public Servant
- **Deputy Minister:** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Michelle_Koski]]
- **Cabinet Minister (per Nov 20, 2026 swearing-in):** Ian Paton (Delta South MLA)

## Structural Mandate
Oversees B.C.'s agriculture, food production, biosecurity, Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) policy interface, and provincial agrifood funding programs. (Previously titled *Ministry of Agriculture and Food*.)
