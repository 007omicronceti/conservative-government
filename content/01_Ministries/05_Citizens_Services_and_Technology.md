# Ministry of Citizens' Services and Technology

## Assigned Senior Public Servant
- **Deputy Minister:** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Silas_Brownsey]]

## Structural Mandate
Manages government digital infrastructure, IT procurement, provincial data governance, Freedom of Information (FOIPPA) administration, Service BC, and provincial real estate/connectivity initiatives. (Expanded title from *Citizens' Services* to explicitly include *Technology*.)
