# Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure

## Leadership Roster (Effective Nov 20, 2026)
- **Minister:** [[02_Cabinet/Trevor_Halford]]
- **Parliamentary Secretary for Infrastructure:** Misty Van Popta
- **Deputy Minister:** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Keith_Godin]]
- **Associate Deputy Ministers:** Paula McKay; Kevin Volk

## What Changed in Doerkson's Restructuring
Re-merged **Transportation** and **Infrastructure** into a single ministry, supported by a dedicated Parliamentary Secretary for Infrastructure.

## What This Ministry Controls
- **Provincial Highways & Bridges:** Highway planning, funding, winter maintenance contracts, and slide/flood repairs.
- **Major Capital Infrastructure:** Provincial infrastructure delivery alongside Infrastructure BC.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **Highway 97 Cariboo Connector:** Four-laning, passing lanes, bridges, and slide repairs through 100 Mile House and Williams Lake—see [[04_Dossiers/Cariboo_Chilcotin/Dossier_Highway97_Cariboo_Connector]].
- **Rural Road Maintenance:** Highway maintenance contractors and emergency detour resilience during wildfire and flood seasons.
