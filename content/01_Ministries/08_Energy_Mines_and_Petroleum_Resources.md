# Ministry of Energy, Mines and Petroleum Resources

## Leadership Roster (Effective Nov 20, 2026)
- **Minister:** [[02_Cabinet/Larry_Neufeld]]
- **Minister of State for Mining:** [[02_Cabinet/Pete_Davis]]
- **Deputy Minister:** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Nate_Amann-Blake]]
- **Associate Deputy Minister:** Chris Trumpy

## What Changed in Doerkson's Restructuring
Premier [[02_Cabinet/Lorne_Doerkson]] consolidated energy, mining, and petroleum resources back into a single ministry—replacing the previous split between *Energy and Climate Solutions* and *Mining and Critical Minerals*—with a dedicated **Minister of State for Mining**.

## What This Ministry Controls
- **Mine Permitting & Regulation:** Mines Act permits, mineral tenure, reclamation bonding, and mine safety.
- **Power & Transmission:** BC Hydro policy, industrial power hookups, and grid capacity for rural mines and mills.
- **Oil, Gas & Petroleum:** Upstream energy, pipelines, and royalties.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **Major Mine Files:** Oversees [[04_Dossiers/Cariboo_Chilcotin/Dossier_Cariboo_Gold_Project]] and [[04_Dossiers/Cariboo_Chilcotin/Dossier_Yellowhead_Copper_100Mile]].
- **Grid & Transmission:** Controls power-line approvals and BC Hydro capacity along the Yellowhead transmission corridor.
