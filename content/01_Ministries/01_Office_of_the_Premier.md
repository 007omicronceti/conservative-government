# Office of the Premier

## Assigned Senior Public Servants
- **Deputy Minister to the Premier, Cabinet Secretary and Head of the Public Service:** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Kim_Henderson]]
- **Deputy Minister, Policy and Coordination:** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Tom_McCarthy]]

## Structural Mandate
Central executive hub of the B.C. provincial government. Oversees Cabinet operations, strategic policy coordination across all 18 line ministries, intergovernmental relations, and direction of the B.C. Public Service.
