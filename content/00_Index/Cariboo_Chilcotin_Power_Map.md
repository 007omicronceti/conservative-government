# Cariboo-Chilcotin & 100 Mile House — Provincial Power Map

## Regional Political Context
- **Premier's Home Riding:** [[02_Cabinet/Lorne_Doerkson]] (Cariboo-Chilcotin) is B.C.'s first Premier from the Interior since Bill Bennett (1986).
- **Notable Exclusion:** Neighboring Prince George–North Cariboo MLA **Sheldon Clare** (Quesnel) was left out of cabinet (reported as a Kerry-Lynne Findlay loyalist), leaving Doerkson and nearby Interior ministers (**Kiel Giddens**, **Peter Milobar**, **Ward Stamer**, **Scott McInnis**, **Donegal Wilson**) as the regional decision-makers.
