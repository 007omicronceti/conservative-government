# DRIPA Repeal & Cariboo-Chilcotin Aboriginal Title
* **DRIPA Repeal:** Government campaigned on repealing DRIPA over legal/court concerns, drawing pushback from First Nations leaders over litigation and conflict risks.
* **Cariboo Nations:** Directly affects consultation, Crown land/tenures, and title negotiations with **Tsilhqot'in**, **Secwepemc**, and **Ulkatcho** First Nations.
