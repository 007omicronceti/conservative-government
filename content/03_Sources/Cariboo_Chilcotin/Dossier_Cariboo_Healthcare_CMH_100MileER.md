# Deep Dive: Highway 97, Cariboo Road Recovery & Quesnel Interconnector

## Active Capital & Slide-Recovery Projects (November 2026)
- **Highway 97 at Cottonwood Hill ($37.5M Phase 3):** 18 km north of Quesnel. Phases 1 (groynes) and 2 (realignment/crest unloading) complete. Phase 3 downslope pile-driving stabilization awarded to **Dawson Civil Ltd.** (Jan 7, 2026); scheduled for full completion in **Spring 2027**.
- **Cariboo Road Recovery Network (9+ Slide Sites):**
  - *Highway 20 at Hodgson/Dog Creek Road:* Dog Creek erosion mitigation complete; ancient Hodgson Slide geotechnical & surface water work ongoing.
  - *Blackwater Road at Knickerbocker Road:* Realignment and bank armouring slated to begin in 2026 (Knickerbocker & North Fraser slides).
  - *Bastin Road at Bastin Hill:* Open to single-lane traffic; construction underway to restore two lanes.
  - *Other Active/Completed Sites:* Quesnel-Hixon Rd (closed/under construction), Quesnel-Hydraulic Rd (under construction), Kersley-Dale Landing Rd (closed/under construction), Hwy 97 at Cuisson Creek (completed/hydroseeding), Soda Creek-MacAlister Rd (completed), Durrell Rd (permanently closed; drainage/turnarounds through Sept 2026).

## Major Decision Gates for the New Cabinet
- **Quesnel North–South Interconnector (2027 Bridge Deadline):** Highway 97 crosses the narrow, two-lane 1961 Quesnel River Bridge (steel truss with height restriction) and CN Rail Overhead Bridge—both in poor condition, with the Rail Overhead Bridge slated for refurbishment in **2027**. The 4-lane Interconnector business case shows a **1.16 benefit-cost ratio** ($281.6M incremental benefits vs. $243.6M do-nothing cost; $310.6M GDP impact) and includes **$32.7M for First Nations consultation/accommodation** tied to DRIPA.
- **Municipal Safety & Upgrade Lobby (2026 NCLGA Resolution O4):** Sponsored by the **Cariboo Regional District** and supported by Prince George (Apr 29, 2026 report, Doc #807773) pressing for Highway 97 safety, maintenance, and passing-lane upgrades.
- **Leadership Watchpoint:** Newly reassigned DM [[02_People/Keith_Godin]] (moving from MCFD) and Minister [[02_Cabinet/Trevor_Halford]] inherit these capital files just ahead of the 2027 spring budget cycle overseen by [[02_Cabinet/Peter_Milobar]].
