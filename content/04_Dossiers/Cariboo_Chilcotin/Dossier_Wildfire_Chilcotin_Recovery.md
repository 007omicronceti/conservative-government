# Wildfire & Chilcotin Landslide Recovery
* **Chilcotin Landslide:** Following the July 2024 Chilcotin River landslide and ongoing slope monitoring, Tsilhqot'in National Government, Tl'esqox, and agencies have tracked sediment, salmon runs, and corridor stability.
* **Wildfire & Recovery:** Fuel-reduction work, emergency readiness, and recovery across CRD, 100 Mile House, Williams Lake, and the Chilcotin.
