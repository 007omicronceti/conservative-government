# 100 Mile Wildfire Fuel Breaks & Chilcotin River Landslide Recovery

## Active 100 Mile House Wildfire Risk Reduction (Winter 2026/27)
- **Watch Lake Interface Fuel Break:** 39.4 ha total project (3 ha pile burning; ~100 piles, ignitions Nov 5, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027), 25 km SE of 100 Mile House adjacent to Watch Lake and Green Lake residences.
- **Mirage Lake Landscape Fuel Break:** 261 ha total project (~138 ha pile burning, Dec 2026 – Mar 31, 2027), 13 km NW of 100 Mile House and 6 km west of 108 Mile Ranch on Tatton McKinley FSR (smoke/flames visible from Highway 97).
- **Policy Context:** Premier [[02_Cabinet/Lorne_Doerkson]] previously introduced *Carter's Law* (Wildfire Act amendments on respiratory health/smoke). Operational wildfire prevention sits under Forests ([[02_Cabinet/Kiel_Giddens]] / [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Janis_Simpkins]]) while evacuation and disaster response sit under Emergency Management ([[02_Cabinet/Ward_Stamer]] / [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Lorie_Hrycuik]]).

## Chilcotin River (Tŝilhqox) Landslide Ongoing Recovery
- **Farwell Canyon Slope Stabilization:** Completed in **May 2026** by the Tŝilhqot'in National Government (TNG) to prevent secondary rockfall barriers to salmon passage downstream of the July 30, 2024 slide.
- **Tripartite Monitoring (TNG / BC / DFO):** Ongoing sonar fish counts (Hanceville) and turbidity/discharge monitoring (Farwell Canyon) continue through 2026 to track multi-year Sockeye and Chinook recovery.
