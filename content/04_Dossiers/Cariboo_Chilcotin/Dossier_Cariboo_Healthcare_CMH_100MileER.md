# Cariboo Healthcare: CMH ($367M) & 100 Mile House ER
* **100 Mile District General Hospital ER (555 Cedar Ave, 100 Mile House):** Saw 28 temporary closures totalling 377 hours in 2025 and ongoing disruptions into early 2026 (including Jan 5, 2026).
* **Cariboo Memorial Hospital (CMH) Redevelopment (Williams Lake):** $367M project planned to add 53 inpatient beds, tracked through Treasury Board and provincial budgets.
* **Accountability:** Premier [[02_Cabinet/Lorne_Doerkson|Lorne Doerkson]] pledged to keep rural ERs open and launched a 100-day *Patients First* health review with [[02_Cabinet/Ryan_Peterson|Ryan Peterson]] and [[02_Cabinet/Peter_Milobar|Peter Milobar]].
