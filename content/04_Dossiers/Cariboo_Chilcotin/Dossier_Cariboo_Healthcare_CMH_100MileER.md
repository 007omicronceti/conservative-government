# Cariboo Memorial Hospital ($367M) & 100 Mile House ER

## Capital Redevelopment & Emergency Staffing Status
- **Cariboo Memorial Hospital (CMH) Redevelopment — Williams Lake:**
  - **Phase 1 (New Addition):** Began April 2023; completion scheduled late 2026 / early 2027. Adds a 23-space emergency department (+12 spaces), two trauma bays, two specialized procedure rooms, ambulance carport, and separate public entrance.
  - **Phase 2 (Existing Hospital Renovations):** Scheduled to start fall 2026 and complete early 2029.
  - **CMH Emergency Staffing:** Interior Health CEO Sylvia Weir stated in April 2026 that CMH ER staffing stabilized with reduced locum reliance and new site leadership.
- **100 Mile District General Hospital Emergency Department:**
  - Experienced over a dozen temporary ER closures between January and April 8, 2026 due to physician and nursing shortages.
  - In May 2026, Interior Health formed a joint task force (local government, Indigenous communities, Cariboo Chilcotin Regional Hospital District, Division of Family Practice) pursuing virtual physician trials, alternative 8-hour shift schedules, physician recruitment, same-day primary care access, and expanded housing/relocation assistance.
