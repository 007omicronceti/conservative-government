# Deep Dive: DRIPA Repeal, Aboriginal Title & Cariboo-Chilcotin Land Certainty

## Political Mandate & Legislative Record
- **Premier's Campaign Pledge:** Premier [[02_Cabinet/Lorne_Doerkson]] pledged during the 2026 election to repeal the *Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples Act* (DRIPA), flagged by the First Nations Leadership Council in its October 21, 2026 election package.
- **MIRR Minister's Prior Statements:** On April 13, 2026, incoming Indigenous Relations Minister [[02_Cabinet/Scott_McInnis]] demanded in the Legislature during debate over the *Gitxaała* mineral-rights case: *"Will this NDP government do the right thing for B.C. and repeal the Declaration Act once and for all?"*

## Cariboo-Chilcotin Operational Impact
- **Title & Resource Permitting Intersection:** The Cariboo-Chilcotin encompasses the *Tsilhqot'in Nation v. British Columbia* Aboriginal title area and active Secwépemc resource/revenue-sharing agreements. Any repeal of DRIPA or Section 8.1 of the *Interpretation Act* will directly test permitting timelines for forestry ([[02_Cabinet/Kiel_Giddens]]), Yellowhead Copper / Cariboo Gold ([[02_Cabinet/Larry_Neufeld]]), and the Quesnel Interconnector ($32.7M budgeted for First Nations accommodation).
- **Legal & Bureaucratic Execution:** Legal drafting and litigation defense will be led by freshman AG [[02_Cabinet/Caroline_Elliott]] and external DM [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Geoff_Cowper]], while day-to-day Nation-to-Nation negotiations remain anchored by retained MIRR DM [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Alex_MacLennan]].
