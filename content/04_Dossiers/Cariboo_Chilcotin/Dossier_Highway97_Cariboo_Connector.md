# Highway 97, Cariboo Road Recovery & Quesnel Interconnector

## Active Capital & Slide-Recovery Projects
- **Highway 97 at Cottonwood Hill ($37.5M Phase 3):** 18 km north of Quesnel. Phases 1 (groynes) and 2 (realignment/crest unloading) complete. Phase 3 downslope pile-driving stabilization awarded to **Dawson Civil Ltd.** (Jan 7, 2026); scheduled for completion in **Spring 2027**.
- **Cariboo Road Recovery Network (9+ Slide Sites):**
  - *Highway 20 at Hodgson/Dog Creek Road:* Dog Creek erosion mitigation complete; Hodgson Slide geotechnical and surface water work ongoing.
  - *Blackwater Road at Knickerbocker Road:* Realignment and bank armouring slated to begin in 2026 (Knickerbocker and North Fraser slides).
  - *Bastin Road at Bastin Hill:* Open to single-lane traffic; construction underway to restore two lanes.
  - *Other Active/Completed Sites:* Quesnel-Hixon Rd (closed/under construction), Quesnel-Hydraulic Rd (under construction), Kersley-Dale Landing Rd (closed/under construction), Hwy 97 at Cuisson Creek (completed/hydroseeding), Soda Creek-MacAlister Rd (completed), Durrell Rd (permanently closed; drainage/turnarounds through Sept 2026).

## Capital Decisions & Municipal Priorities
- **Quesnel North–South Interconnector:** Highway 97 crosses the two-lane 1961 Quesnel River Bridge and CN Rail Overhead Bridge (slated for refurbishment in **2027**). The 4-lane Interconnector business case reports a **1.16 benefit-cost ratio** ($281.6M incremental benefits vs. $243.6M do-nothing cost) and includes **$32.7M for First Nations consultation and accommodation**.
- **Municipal & NCLGA Resolutions:** Cariboo Regional District and City of Prince George (Apr 29, 2026 report, Doc #807773) and 2026 NCLGA resolutions pressing for Highway 97 passing lanes, winter maintenance standards, and cellular connectivity along the corridor.
