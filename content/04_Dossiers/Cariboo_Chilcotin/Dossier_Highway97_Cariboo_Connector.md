# Highway 97 Cariboo Connector & Corridor Infrastructure

## Plain-English Summary
Highway 97 is the sole north–south lifeline for freight, logging trucks, medical transfers, and residents between Cache Creek, 100 Mile House, Williams Lake, and Quesnel. Four-laning phases, slide-prone sections, and winter maintenance standards remain top regional priorities pushed by local councils and the North Central Local Government Association (NCLGA).
