# Critical Issue Audit: The Yellowhead Transmission Line Battle

## Ground-Truth Facts
1. **The Official Preferred Route:** Taseko Mines' 110-km, 230-kV high-voltage line running east from the 100 Mile BC Hydro substation directly across private Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR) parcels and ranches in Area L / Interlakes. Over 500 residents petitioned against this routing.
2. **The McLean Delegation (March 24, 2026):** Jake McLean presented an alternate ~130-km northern corridor to 100 Mile Council, proposing to utilize the existing abandoned Hendrix Lake Mine power line easement, route south of Canim Lake and Mahood Lake, and connect to Clearwater via Forest Service Roads. This route was designed to protect private ALR land and create a Wells Gray tourism loop.
3. **The Rapid Coordinated Pushback (April 2, 2026):**
   * Mayor Maureen Pinkney cross-examined McLean on First Nations consultation.
   * CRD Chair Margo Wagner phoned Pinkney the morning news broke and contacted the press to counter McLean's statements, invoking her authority as NDIT Chair to claim regional development trusts would not fund such alternatives.
   * CCCTA CEO Amy Thacker publicly stated the tourism association had taken no stance on funding feasibility.
4. **Director Support:** CRD Area L Director Eric De Vries formally wrote to provincial ministers Adrian Dix and Jagrup Brar calling the McLean alternative technically viable to prevent utility fragmentation of the Interlakes.
