# 🏥 Infrastructure Dossier: 100 Mile District General Hospital ER Closures

## The Core Conflict
* **The Crisis:** Persistent, rolling temporary emergency room closures throughout the region due to acute staffing shortages, physician burnout, and systemic under-resourcing across Interior Health.
* **The Impact:** Forces emergency medical traffic down the highway corridor to Cariboo Memorial Hospital in Williams Lake, putting critical minutes and patient outcomes at risk across the South Cariboo.

## The Governance Shield
* **The Local Leadership Posture:** District leadership and council incumbents routinely hide behind the "provincial/Interior Health jurisdiction" shield, treating life-safety emergency closures as someone else's paperwork problem rather than an urgent mandate for vocal, aggressive municipal advocacy.
