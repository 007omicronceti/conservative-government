# Yellowhead Copper Project & 100 Mile House Substation

## Project Overview & 100 Mile House Nexus
- **Mine Scale:** Proposed 90,000 tonnes-per-day open-pit copper-gold-silver mine (42,350 ha of mineral claims + mining lease valid to June 2050) near Vavenby/Birch Island.
- **The 100 Mile House Tie-In:** Requires a **~110 km, 230 kV dedicated transmission line** connecting the mine site to the **existing BC Hydro substation at 100 Mile House**. After four years of review, **BC Hydro determined that 100 Mile House is the only viable point of interconnection**.
- **Land & Tenure Intersections (CRD):** Crosses undisturbed Crown land, **Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR)** parcels, private land, forest harvest tenures, range/grazing tenures, guide outfitting/trapline tenures, Old-Growth Management Areas (OGMAs), and mapped caribou and badger habitat.

## Regulatory Status (November 2026)
- **BC EAO Process Planning:** Public comment period on the draft Process Order closed **October 22, 2026**.
- **New Cabinet Decision Gates:** Doerkson's cabinet now oversees the final Process Order, cumulative-effects scope (Environment/EAO), BC Hydro interconnection & mine permitting (Energy/Mines), Occupant License to Cut (Forests), and Crown land/ALR authorizations (WLNRO & Agriculture).
