# November 19–24, 2026 Government Transition Orders in Council (`OIC 418` – `OIC 438`)

Following the swearing-in of Premier [[02_Cabinet/Lorne_Doerkson]] and the B.C. Conservative Executive Council on **November 20, 2026**, Cabinet issued 21 Orders in Council restructuring ministries, statutory committees, deputy ministers, and political staff.

## 1. Key Governance & Committee Structures

### Treasury Board ([[05_Primary_Sources/OIC/OIC_0424_2026|OIC 424/2026]])
- **Vice Chair:** [[02_Cabinet/Kiel_Giddens|Hon. Kiel Giddens]] (Minister of Forests; Prince George–Mackenzie)
- **Members:** [[02_Cabinet/Bruce_Banman|Hon. Bruce Banman]], [[02_Cabinet/Carson_Binda|Hon. Carson Binda]], [[02_Cabinet/Trevor_Halford|Hon. Trevor Halford]], [[02_Cabinet/Scott_McInnis|Hon. Scott McInnis]], [[02_Cabinet/Cathie_Ounsted|Hon. Cathie Ounsted]], [[02_Cabinet/Ryan_Peterson|Hon. Ryan Peterson]], [[02_Cabinet/Aa_liya_Warbus|Hon. A'a:liya Warbus]], Kevin Acton, Nicholas Cartmell, Sacha Peter, Bryan Tepper, Misty Van Popta.
- **Secretary to Treasury Board ([[05_Primary_Sources/OIC/OIC_0429_2026|OIC 429/2026]]):** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Heather_Wood|Heather Wood]] (Deputy Minister of Finance), replacing Douglas Scott and Acting Secretary [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Jonathan_Dube|Jonathan Dube]].

### Environment and Land Use Committee — ELUC ([[05_Primary_Sources/OIC/OIC_0423_2026|OIC 423/2026]])
- **Chair:** [[02_Cabinet/Donegal_Wilson|Hon. Donegal Wilson]] (Minister of Water, Land and Natural Resource Operations)
- **Members:** [[02_Cabinet/Pete_Davis|Hon. Pete Davis]] (Mining), [[02_Cabinet/Kiel_Giddens|Hon. Kiel Giddens]] (Forests), [[02_Cabinet/Larry_Neufeld|Hon. Larry Neufeld]] (Energy & Mines), [[02_Cabinet/Ian_Paton|Hon. Ian Paton]] (Agriculture), [[02_Cabinet/Aa_liya_Warbus|Hon. A'a:liya Warbus]] (Environment), Mike Hicks, Darryl Kropp, Garry Purewal, Jody Toor.
- **OSINT Note:** ELUC holds statutory override authority under s. 2 of the *Environment and Land Use Act* and is dominated by Interior/resource ministers overseeing Cariboo-Chilcotin mining, forestry, and Crown land files.

### Reconstituted Cabinet Committees & Working Groups ([[05_Primary_Sources/OIC/OIC_0425_2026|OIC 425/2026]])
Amends B.C. Reg. 267/2022 to establish seven bodies:
1. **Priorities and Planning Committee**
2. **Working Group on Major Projects and Investment**
3. **Working Group on Wildfire Response**
4. **Committee on Indigenous Partnerships**
5. **Committee on Health and Social Services**
6. **Committee on Innovative Economy**
7. **Committee on Public Safety and Justice**
