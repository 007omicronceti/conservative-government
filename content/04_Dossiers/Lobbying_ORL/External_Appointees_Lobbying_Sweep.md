# External Appointees & BC ORL Lobbying Sweep

## Plain-English Summary
When a new government takes office, watching **who comes in from the private sector** and **who is actively lobbying senior Deputy Ministers** via the **B.C. Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists (ORL)** is the fastest way to spot policy shifts before they hit the news.

## Priority External Appointees to Track
- **[[03_Deputy_Ministers/Geoff_Cowper]] (Deputy Attorney General):** Appointed Nov 20, 2026 directly from private practice at **Fasken**. Key figure on DRIPA, Aboriginal title litigation, and constitutional strategy.
- **[[03_Deputy_Ministers/Janis_Simpkins]] (Forests):** Tracked for prior industry ties and ORL filings affecting regional timber and Tolko tenure files.

## Active Deputy Minister Lobbying Touchpoints (2026 ORL)
- **[[03_Deputy_Ministers/Lori_Halls]] (Environment / EAO):** Lobbied by B.C. Wildlife Federation, Guide Outfitters Association of B.C., and CPAWS-BC on wildlife and land-use files.
- **[[03_Deputy_Ministers/Heather_Wood]] (Finance):** Lobbied Oct 2026 on provincial budgets, procurement, infrastructure, and outsourcing.
- **[[03_Deputy_Ministers/Kevin_Jardine]] (Deputy Minister):** Lobbied Aug–Sept 2026 by industrial firms on environmental protection and permitting.
