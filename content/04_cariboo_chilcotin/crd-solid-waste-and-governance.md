## Overview & Timeline
- Updated 10-year Solid Waste Management Plan approved by the Ministry of Environment and Parks in **March 2025** after two consultation rounds and Solid Waste Advisory Committee input.
- Covers waste prevention/reduction, diversion hubs and curbside/commercial recycling, organics/food-waste diversion, disposal bans, user-pay/resource recovery, rural/attended sites, invasive plants, and wildfire fuel reduction.

## Primary & Public Records to Link
- CRD Solid Waste Management Plan (`cariboord.ca`) and 103/105 Mile curbside collection rollout records.
- 2010–2013 Advisory Committee rosters/agendas/consultation reports, 2023 Solid Waste & Invasive Plants Management Business Plan, and CRD board/committee minutes.

## Function 1008 & 2026 Business Plan Metrics
- **Facility Footprint:** 33 refuse sites across the CRD—18 transfer stations and 15 landfills—managed under Solid Waste Management Service (Function 1008).
- **Reserves & Rollouts:** Capital reserves at $4,886,143 after the 2025 contribution and post-closure/closure reserves at $4,933,128 before the 2025 contribution, alongside curbside collection expansion (Red Bluff and 103/105 Mile launched Oct. 2025; South Lakeside and Dog Creek slated for 2026), food-waste drop-offs, and disposal-ban load audits.

## Rural Site Access, Diversion Centre & ICI Pilots
- Evaluating gated access and adjusted operating hours for unstaffed rural refuse sites under Function 1008.
- Advancing feasibility work for a one-stop regional Diversion Centre alongside Industrial/Commercial/Institutional (ICI) packaging and paper recycling pilots.
