## CRD Solid Waste & South Cariboo Functions
- Built on the **March 2025** Solid Waste Management Plan update, including curbside bylaws (Bylaw No. 5560 in Feb. 2026; Bylaw No. 5588 adding Dog Creek and South Lakeside in Aug. 2026), curbside collection rollouts, organics/diversion, and landfill/transfer site management.
- South Cariboo Economic Development (Function 1017) tracks regional tourism, trails/parks, and NDIT grant competition alongside CRD regional services.

## 100 Mile House & 100 Mile Development Corporation
- Mayor David Jurek and Councillors Donna Barnett, Erin Filtness, Ralf Holzapfel, and David Mingo were sworn in on **November 10, 2026**, followed by November 24, 2026 100 Mile Development Corporation and council/committee meetings.
- Nov. 24, 2026 Development Corp agenda items include David Jurek becoming Chair (following Maureen Ann Pinkney's resignation), acting-chair terms through Nov. 2030, and signing/signatory authority resolutions with senior municipal staff.

## Provincial Mandate Alignment (Nov. 20, 2026 Swearing-In / Nov. 24, 2026 Mandates)
- Premier Lorne Doerkson's mandate letters pair Treasury Board fiscal discipline and regulatory reduction (including Minister of State Carson Binda's red-tape mandate) with resource permitting, forestry and wildfire priorities, municipal OCP housing autonomy, and rural healthcare support.

## Nov. 2026 South Cariboo & Mandate Cross-Check
- District of 100 Mile House council, Committee of the Whole, and 100 Mile Development Corporation meetings ran Nov. 10 and Nov. 24, 2026 alongside the CRD's 33-site solid waste network and March 2025 SWMP update.
- Premier Lorne Doerkson's Nov. 24, 2026 mandate letters set five pillars with measurable targets, Treasury Board spending checks, fiscal/tax review panels, and health and public-safety shifts.
