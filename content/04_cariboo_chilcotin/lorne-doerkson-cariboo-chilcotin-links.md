## Nov. 24, 2026 Mandate Cross-Walk for Cariboo-Chilcotin
- **Municipal & Housing Autonomy:** Restoring local government say over housing location, form, and character aligned with Official Community Plans (Local Government, Housing, Sport and Culture).
- **Forestry & Wildfire Readiness:** 45-million-cubic-metre annual harvest permit floor, single-window consultation/permitting, old-growth deferral resolution, Community Protection Forestry, $400M Wildfire Aircraft Fleet Expansion, and $100M Wildfire Innovation Fund (Forests & Emergency Management).
- **Rural Healthcare & Safety:** $50M Rural Health Emergency Fund, keeping rural ERs and maternity wards open, ending prescribed safe supply, and expanding policing/community safety supports (Health, Social Development, and Public Safety).

## Nov. 24–25, 2026 Mandate Rollout Details
- **Five-Goal Structure & Accountability:** Mandate letters tie priorities to *An Affordable B.C.*, *A Prosperous B.C.*, *A Healthy B.C.*, *A Safer B.C.*, and *A Strong and Proud B.C.*, requiring measurable outcomes, delivery dates, cross-ministry coordination, and Treasury Board review for new spending.
- **Fiscal, Tax & Climate Shifts:** Pairs Treasury Board discipline and public-service staffing reviews (with independent fiscal and tax review panels) with repealing CleanBC and ZEV sales quotas, alongside health wait-time guarantees and ending safe supply.

## Nov. 25, 2026 Mandate Letter Coverage
- Premier Lorne Doerkson's letters group priorities under five goals (Affordable, Prosperous, Healthy, Safer, Strong and Proud B.C.) with measurable outcomes and delivery dates.
- Includes independent fiscal and tax review panels (tax review within 180 days), health-care wait-time guarantees, ending safe supply, reviewing overdose prevention sites, and expanding drug courts.

## Nov. 25, 2026 Specific Directives
- Attorney General Caroline Elliott tasked with introducing a Property Rights Defence Act.
- Education Minister Lynne Block directed to replace SOGI 123, alongside a government-wide review of recent provincial policy changes and restoring the online provincial staff directory.
