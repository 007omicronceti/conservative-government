# Kaye Krishna (Kathryn Krishna)

## Current Role
- **Title:** Deputy Minister of Children and Family Development
- **Ministry:** [[01_Ministries/04_Children_and_Family_Development]]

## Career & Background Summary
- **Ministry of Education and Child Care (through Oct 2026):** Served as Deputy Minister of Education and Child Care (listed under Kathryn Krishna on the Deputy Ministers' Council).
- **Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing (2019–2020+):** Appointed Deputy Minister effective March 4, 2019, overseeing local government, housing, property assessment policy, and construction standards.
- **City of Vancouver (Pre-2019):** Served as **General Manager of Development Services, Buildings and Licensing** at Vancouver City Hall (reported compensation of $265,743 in 2017 per City Statement of Financial Information).
- **Private Sector & Early Career:** Originally from Ohio. Worked for a decade as a management consultant for major companies across the U.S. and Canada before earning a Master's degree in Urban Planning. Named to the **Urban Land Institute's inaugural 40 Under 40** class in 2014.

## OSINT Watchpoints
- **Portfolio Shift:** Moves laterally from Education & Child Care (and earlier municipal housing/development) into the politically high-risk child welfare portfolio (MCFD).
