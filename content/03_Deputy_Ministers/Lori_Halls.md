# Lori Halls — Deputy Minister & Chief Executive Assessment Officer, Ministry of Environment

**Lori Halls** is a veteran British Columbia public servant appointed on November 20, 2026, as **Deputy Minister and Chief Executive Assessment Officer** for the [[01_Ministries/09_Environment]].

## Why She Matters
Holding both the Deputy Minister title and **Chief Executive Assessment Officer** puts Halls directly in charge of the **Environmental Assessment Office (EAO)**—the provincial gatekeeper that reviews, conditions, and advances major industrial, mining, and infrastructure projects.

## Background & Track Record
- **Previous Portfolio (WLRS):** Served as Deputy Minister of the former Ministry of Water, Land and Resource Stewardship (WLRS) prior to the November 2026 transition, overseeing land-use planning, watershed stewardship, wildlife, and wild salmon/steelhead conservation.
- **Cross-Government Continuity:** Retained in senior deputy leadership across the transition from the NDP to Premier [[02_Cabinet/Lorne_Doerkson]]'s Conservative government.
- **Lobbying & Stakeholder Touchpoints (BC ORL):** Frequently lobbied in her previous WLRS role by conservation, hunting, and resource groups—including the B.C. Wildlife Federation, Guide Outfitters Association of British Columbia, and CPAWS-BC—on wildlife, habitat, and land-use policy.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **Mining Assessments (EAO):** Directly oversees environmental assessment timelines and conditions for major corridor projects, including [[04_Dossiers/Cariboo_Chilcotin/Dossier_Yellowhead_Copper_100Mile]] and [[04_Dossiers/Cariboo_Chilcotin/Dossier_Cariboo_Gold_Project]].
- **Fish, Wildlife & Land-Use Carryover:** Brings direct institutional memory from WLRS on Chilcotin salmon/steelhead recovery and regional land-use files.
