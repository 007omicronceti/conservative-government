# Tom McCarthy

## Current Role
- **Title:** Deputy Minister, Policy and Coordination
- **Ministry:** [[01_Ministries/01_Office_of_the_Premier]]

## Career & Background Summary
- **Retained Senior Executive:** Held the exact same title—Deputy Minister, Policy and Coordination, Office of the Premier—under Premier David Eby's NDP government through 2025–2026 (serving alongside former Deputy Minister to the Premier Shannon Salter).
- **First Nations Governance:** Previously served as **Chief Administrative Officer (CAO) of the Tsawwassen First Nation**, contributing to the BC Assembly of First Nations (BCAFN) *Governance Toolkit* (2011).
- **Education (Flagged for Secondary Identity Confirmation):** SFU School of Public Policy alumni archives list a Tom McCarthy as an MPP graduate with a Bachelor of Public Affairs and Policy Management from Carleton University.

## OSINT Watchpoints
- **Cross-Administration Retention:** Keeping the sitting Policy & Coordination Deputy inside the Premier's Office provides operational continuity on files carried over from the Eby administration.
