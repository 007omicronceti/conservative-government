# Bobbi Plecas

## Current Role
- **Title:** Deputy Minister of Education and Child Care
- **Ministry:** [[01_Ministries/07_Education_and_Child_Care]]
- **Cabinet Minister:** Lynne Block

## Career & Background Summary
- **Ministry of Infrastructure (through Oct 2026):** Served as Deputy Minister of the standalone Ministry of Infrastructure under Minister Bowinn Ma prior to the November 2026 ministry consolidation.
- **Climate Change / CleanBC Architect (July 2017–2020s):** Appointed Deputy Minister, Climate Change in the Ministry of Environment and Climate Change Strategy in July 2017, where she led the cross-government development of the **CleanBC** plan.

## OSINT Watchpoints
- **Major Portfolio Shift:** Moves from capital infrastructure and earlier climate-policy leadership (CleanBC) into Education and Child Care—taking over the seat previously held by Kaye Krishna.
