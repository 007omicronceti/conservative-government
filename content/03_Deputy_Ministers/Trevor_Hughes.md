# Trevor Hughes — Deputy Minister

**Trevor Hughes** is a senior British Columbia Deputy Minister listed on the Deputy Ministers' Council as of November 20, 2026.

## Background & Track Record
- **Post-Secondary & Trades Workforce:** Served as Deputy Minister of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills, representing the province on the **Deputy Ministers & Industry Infrastructure Forum (DMIIF)** alongside the BC Construction Association.
- **Labour & Construction Interface:** Focused on skilled trades training, apprenticeship pipelines, and workforce readiness for major capital projects.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **Skilled Trades & Resource Workforce:** Directly relevant to trades shortages for Cariboo Memorial Hospital construction, Highway 97 upgrades, and mine construction/operations at Cariboo Gold and Yellowhead Copper.
