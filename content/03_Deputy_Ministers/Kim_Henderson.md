# Kim Henderson

## Current Role
- **Title:** Deputy Minister to the Premier, Cabinet Secretary and Head of the Public Service
- **Ministry:** [[01_Ministries/01_Office_of_the_Premier]]
- **Prior Transition Role:** Named Chair of Premier-elect Lorne Doerkson's Government Transition Team on October 25, 2026.

## Career & Background Summary
- **Private Sector & Advisory (Post-2017–2026):** Principal at **Sproat Advising**, an independent consultancy providing advisory services to businesses; Fellow at the **Public Policy Forum**.
- **Top B.C. Civil Servant under Premier Christy Clark (2016–2017):** Appointed Deputy Minister to the Premier, Cabinet Secretary, and Head of the Public Service on February 9, 2016.
- **Ministry of Finance (Pre-2016):** Served as Deputy Minister of Finance and Secretary to Treasury Board immediately prior to taking the Premier's Office post.
- **Ministry of Citizens' Services (2010):** Served as Deputy Minister of Citizens' Services; testified before the Legislative Assembly's Special Committee to Review FIPPA on March 24, 2010 alongside Government CIO Dave Nikolejsin.
- **Education:** Master of Public Administration (MPA), University of Victoria.

## OSINT Watchpoints
- **Continuity with Christy Clark Era:** Direct institutional bridge between the former B.C. Liberal executive bureaucracy and the new Doerkson B.C. Conservative government.
- **Private Consultancy Client List:** As Principal of Sproat Advising prior to returning as Head of the Public Service, monitor conflict-of-interest screens regarding former corporate clients.
