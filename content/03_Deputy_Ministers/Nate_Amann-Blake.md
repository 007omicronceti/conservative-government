# Nate Amann-Blake (Nathaniel Amann-Blake)

## Current Role
- **Title:** Deputy Minister of Energy, Mines and Petroleum Resources
- **Ministry:** [[01_Ministries/08_Energy_Mines_and_Petroleum_Resources]]
- **Cabinet Minister:** Larry Neufeld

## Career & Background Summary
- **Ministry of Mining and Critical Minerals (Nov 2024–Nov 2026):** Appointed Deputy Minister of Mining and Critical Minerals on November 18, 2024 under the Eby government.
- **Expanded Resource Mandate (Nov 2026):** Retained and elevated to lead the newly recombined Energy, Mines and Petroleum Resources ministry, absorbing electricity and oil/gas files alongside mining and permitting reform.

## OSINT Watchpoints
- **Resource Permitting & Major Industry Contact:** Premier Doerkson has prioritized slashing permitting times for major resource projects. Amann-Blake is already the primary bureaucratic contact point for major energy and mining lobbies, including **Shell Canada Limited** and the **Mining Association of BC**.
