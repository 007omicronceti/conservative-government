# Jonathan Dubé — Deputy Minister

**Jonathan Dubé** is a senior British Columbia Deputy Minister listed on the Deputy Ministers' Council as of November 20, 2026.

## Background & Track Record
- **Treasury Board & Fiscal Gatekeeper:** Previously served as Associate Deputy Minister of Finance and Deputy Secretary to Treasury Board (Treasury Board Staff), giving him direct control over provincial budget submissions and capital project approvals.
- **Infrastructure Oversight:** Represented Treasury Board on the **Deputy Ministers & Industry Infrastructure Forum (DMIIF)** alongside Infrastructure BC and the Ministries of Infrastructure and Transportation.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **Capital Project Approvals:** Treasury Board staff review and sign off on business cases for major regional spending—including hospital redevelopments, Highway 97 corridor upgrades, and rural infrastructure grants.
