# Alex MacLennan — Deputy Minister, Indigenous Relations and Reconciliation

**Alex MacLennan** serves on British Columbia's Deputy Ministers' Council as Deputy Minister for the [[01_Ministries/13_Indigenous_Relations_and_Reconciliation]], continuing in senior leadership following Premier [[02_Cabinet/Lorne_Doerkson]]'s November 20, 2026 cabinet transition.

## Why He Matters
As the top non-elected official in Indigenous Relations and Reconciliation (IRR), MacLennan oversees treaty negotiations, land and resource revenue-sharing agreements, and provincial-First Nations consultation frameworks.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **Title, Tenure & DRIPA:** Directly involved in provincial legal and policy responses affecting Aboriginal title, land-use certainty, and any legislative changes to DRIPA—see [[04_Dossiers/Cariboo_Chilcotin/Dossier_DRIPA_Repeal_Cariboo_Title]].
- **Resource Project Agreements:** IRR negotiates economic and benefit-sharing frameworks with First Nations along the Highway 97 corridor that directly affect mining and forestry timelines.
