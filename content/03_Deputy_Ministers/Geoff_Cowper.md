# Geoff Cowper, KC (Geoffrey Cowper)

## Current Role
- **Title:** Deputy Minister of Attorney General
- **Ministry:** [[01_Ministries/03_Attorney_General]]

## Career & Background Summary
- **Private Legal Practice:** Senior Partner in Litigation and Dispute Resolution at **Fasken** (Vancouver). Called to the British Columbia Bar in 1982 and the Yukon Bar in 1987.
- **Practice Specialties:** Commercial litigation, class actions, appellate practice, administrative and public law, **Political Law** (election finance, lobbyist registration, public sector conflict of interest), and Government Relations/Crisis Communication.
- **Major Crown & Utility Representations:**
  - Represented **BC Hydro and Power Authority** on Site C project approval litigation (2022).
  - Represented **FortisBC Energy Inc.** in pipeline abandonment disputes with the City of Coquitlam (2021) and BC Utilities Commission (BCUC) inquiries into municipal energy utilities (2021–present).
- **Provincial Commissions & Justice Reform:**
  - **Chair, BC Justice Reform Initiative (2012–2015):** Authored the landmark Cowper Report on criminal justice delays and charge assessment reform (delivered a 4-year update report on October 19, 2016).
  - **Commission Counsel:** Davies Commission on First Nations and Policing (2010); Matkin Commission on Securities Regulation (1994); Hall Commission on First Nations Policy (1987).
- **Boards & Non-Profits:** Director & Chair of the **Legal Services Society** (B.C. legal aid, 1998–2008); Director, **Street to Home Foundation** (2008–present); Chair, Fellow and Provincial Chair, American College of Trial Lawyers (2005–2007).
- **Education:** LLB, University of British Columbia (1980).

## OSINT Watchpoints
- **External Private-Sector Appointment:** Unlike most retained career deputies on this roster, Cowper is a direct appointment from a major corporate law firm (Fasken).
- **Potential Conflict Screens:** Extensive prior representation of BC Hydro, FortisBC, and corporate class-action/securities clients will require formal recusal walls on files touching those entities.
- **Justice Reform Mandate:** Appointment signals an aggressive push on court backlogs, bail/charge assessment standards, and prosecutorial policy echoing his 2012–2016 Justice Reform reports.
