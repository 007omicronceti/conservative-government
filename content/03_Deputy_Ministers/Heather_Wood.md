# Heather Wood — Deputy Minister, Ministry of Finance

**Heather Wood** serves as **Deputy Minister, Ministry of Finance**, working directly under Finance Minister [[02_Cabinet/Peter_Milobar]] following the November 20, 2026 cabinet transition.

## Background & Track Record
- **Top Fiscal Bureaucrat:** Oversees provincial budget preparation, debt management, tax policy implementation, and Treasury Board operations.
- **Lobbying & Infrastructure Touchpoints (BC ORL):** Lobbied in October 2026 on finances and budgets, government procurement, infrastructure, privatization/outsourcing, and transportation.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **Spring 2027 Budget Execution:** Translates cabinet's tax-cut and capital-review priorities into actual spending envelopes for rural hospitals, Highway 97, and local government grants.
