# Silas Brownsey

## Current Role
- **Title:** Deputy Minister of Citizens' Services and Technology
- **Ministry:** [[01_Ministries/05_Citizens_Services_and_Technology]]

## Career & Background Summary
- **Ministry of Tourism, Arts, Culture and Sport (through Aug 2026):** Served as Deputy Minister of Tourism, Arts, Culture and Sport while also sitting on the **Deputy Ministers' Committee on Digital and Data (DMCDD)** (co-author of its March 2025 Terms of Reference governing IT investment, data management, and Connected Services BC).
- **Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing (2020):** Served as Associate Deputy Minister, Strategic Planning and Land Use (working under then-DM Kaye Krishna).
- **Ministry of Jobs, Trade and Technology / InBC (2019):** Served on the Board of Directors of the **BC Immigrant Investment Fund** (BCIIF).
- **Federal Public Service:** Began his career with the federal government at **Indian and Northern Affairs Canada (INAC)**.

## OSINT Watchpoints
- **Digital & Data Governance:** Already embedded in the province's internal digital-governance architecture (DMCDD) prior to taking over the newly renamed *Citizens' Services and Technology* portfolio.
