# Peter Pokorny

## Current Role
- **Title:** Deputy Minister of Health
- **Ministry:** [[01_Ministries/12_Health]]
- **Cabinet Minister:** Ryan Peterson
- **Replaces:** Cynthia Johansen (former Deputy Minister of Health)

## Career & Background Summary
- **Ministry of Energy and Climate Solutions (through Oct 2026):** Served as Deputy Minister of Energy and Climate Solutions under the Eby government, and sat on the province's **Climate Solutions Council**.
- **BC Energy Regulator (BCER):** Appointed as a Director of the Board of the British Columbia Energy Regulator on February 10, 2025 (term originally set to end February 17, 2028).

## OSINT Watchpoints
- **Major Portfolio Shift:** Moves from Energy/Climate Solutions into B.C.'s largest and most politically sensitive budget line—Health—replacing outgoing Health DM Cynthia Johansen.
