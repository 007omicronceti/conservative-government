## Current Role
- **Title:** Deputy Minister of Health
- **Ministry:** [[01_Ministries/12_Health]]

## Career & Background Summary
- **Ministry of Energy and Climate Solutions (through Oct 2026):** Served as Deputy Minister of Energy and Climate Solutions under the Eby government, and sat on the province's **Climate Solutions Council**.

## OSINT Watchpoints
