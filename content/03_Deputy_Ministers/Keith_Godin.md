# Keith M. Godin — Deputy Minister

**Keith M. Godin** is a senior British Columbia public servant listed on the Deputy Ministers' Council as of November 20, 2026.

## Background & Track Record
- **Social Service Delivery Review (2026):** Served as Deputy Minister for the Ministry of Children and Family Development (MCFD), where in June–August 2026 he co-led a province-wide engagement with the Community Social Services Employers' Association (CSSEA) to review and coordinate social service delivery across B.C.
- **Cross-Ministry Alignment:** Focused on strategic alignment and integration between child/family services and broader community social service providers.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **Rural Social Services & Contracting:** Decisions on provincial social service coordination, funding distribution, and agency staffing directly affect frontline non-profits and family support agencies in 100 Mile House and Williams Lake.
