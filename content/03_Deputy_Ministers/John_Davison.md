# John Davison — President & CEO, PSEC Secretariat

**John Davison** is **President and Chief Executive Officer of the Public Sector Employers' Council Secretariat (PSEC)** under the [[01_Ministries/10_Finance]], retained on the Deputy Ministers' Council as of November 20, 2026.

## Why He Matters
PSEC sets and enforces provincial bargaining mandates and executive compensation rules across B.C.'s entire broader public sector—including health authorities, school districts, universities, and Crown corporations like WorkSafeBC.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **Healthcare & Education Bargaining:** PSEC mandates govern wage and staffing negotiations for Interior Health (affecting Cariboo Memorial Hospital and 100 Mile District General Hospital) and School District 27.
