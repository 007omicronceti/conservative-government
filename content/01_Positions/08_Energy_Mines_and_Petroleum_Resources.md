# Fazil Mihlar

## Current Role
- **Title:** Deputy Minister of Economic Development and Tourism
- **Ministry:** [[01_Positions/06_Economic_Development_and_Tourism]]
- **Cabinet Minister:** Gavin Dew

## Career & Background Summary
- **Jobs, Economic Development & Innovation (Dec 2022–Nov 2026):** Appointed Deputy Minister of Jobs, Economic Development and Innovation (later Jobs and Economic Growth) on December 7, 2022; retained through the Eby administration and now expanded to include Tourism.
- **Energy, Mines & Low-Carbon Innovation (Pre-Dec 2022):** Served as Deputy Minister overseeing energy, mining, and natural gas policy.
- **Provincial Entry (2013):** Recruited into the B.C. Public Service under Premier Christy Clark's B.C. Liberal government in 2013, joining the Ministry of Natural Gas Development to champion B.C.'s LNG industry.
- **Think Tank & Media Background (Pre-2013):** Former senior director/researcher at the **Fraser Institute** and former **Vancouver Sun** editorial columnist. Known for free-market policy positions, including critiques of environmental assessment processes, minimum wage mandates, and public ownership.

## OSINT Watchpoints
- **Three-Government Survivor:** One of the most ideologically notable senior bureaucrats in Victoria—hired under Christy Clark (BC Liberals), retained for nine years under John Horgan and David Eby (BC NDP), and now positioned as the lead economic deputy for Premier Doerkson's BC Conservatives.
- **Lobbying Activity:** Frequently listed as a lobbied Senior Public Office Holder in BC Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists (ORL) monthly logs (including June 2026).
