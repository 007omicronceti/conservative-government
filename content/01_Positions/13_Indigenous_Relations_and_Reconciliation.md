# Janis Simpkins

## Current Role
- **Title:** Deputy Minister of Forests
- **Ministry:** [[01_Positions/11_Forests]]
- **Cabinet Minister:** Kiel Giddens
- **Replaces:** Makenzie Leine (former Deputy Minister of Forests)

## Career & Background Summary
- **Tolko Industries Ltd. (Private Sector Executive):** Served on Tolko's senior leadership team as **Vice President of Indigenous & Government Relations and Sustainability** (based out of Vernon/BC & Alberta forestry operations).
- **Industry Association Governance:** Served on the Board of Directors of the **Alberta Forest Products Association (AFPA)**.
- **Recognition & Education:** Named one of the **Canadian Forest Industry's Top 10 Under 40** in 2021. Holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from the University of Alberta.

## OSINT Watchpoints
- **Direct Industry-to-Regulator Appointment:** Simpkins is one of the highest-profile external appointments on Doerkson's roster. As recently as **May and July 2025**, she was actively lobbying the exact office she now leads—meeting with outgoing Forests DM Makenzie Leine and BC Timber Sales (BCTS) Executive Director Allan Powelson on behalf of Tolko Industries Ltd.
- **Conflict-of-Interest Screens:** Will require strict conflict-of-interest walls regarding Tolko Industries' timber tenures, stumpage rates, and BCTS allocations.
