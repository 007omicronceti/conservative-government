# Ministry of Economic Development and Tourism

## Assigned Senior Public Servant & Political Leadership
- **Deputy Minister:** [[02_People/Fazil_Mihlar]]
- **Cabinet Minister:** Gavin Dew
- **Associated Ministers:** Carson Binda (Red Tape Reduction); Anna Kindy (Minister of State for Rural Development)

## Structural Mandate
Consolidates provincial economic development, investment attraction, innovation, and tourism portfolios (merging functions formerly split between *Jobs, Economic Development and Innovation* and *Tourism, Arts, Culture and Sport*).
