# Caroline Elliott

## Role & Political Profile
- **Office:** Attorney General (sworn in November 20, 2026)
- **Riding:** West Vancouver-Capilano (freshman MLA; holds a PhD in political science; came within fewer than 100 votes of winning the May 2026 BC Conservative leadership race against Kerry-Lynne Findlay).

## Cariboo-Chilcotin Impact
- Paired with external Deputy Minister [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Geoff_Cowper]], Elliott oversees the provincial legal strategy on any **DRIPA repeal**, ongoing Aboriginal title/rights litigation affecting Cariboo-Chilcotin Crown lands, and criminal justice/bail reform.
