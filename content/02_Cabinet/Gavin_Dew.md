# Gavin Dew

## Role & Political Profile
- **Office:** Minister of Economic Development and Tourism (sworn in November 20, 2026)
- **Junior Ministers:** Carson Binda (Red Tape Reduction) and Anna Kindy (Rural Development).

## Cariboo-Chilcotin Impact
- Paired with veteran DM [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Fazil_Mihlar]] and Rural Development MoS Anna Kindy, Dew controls rural economic transition funding (critical for 100 Mile House post-mill recovery), regulatory red-tape reduction, and Interior tourism marketing.
