# Ward Stamer

## Role & Political Profile
- **Office:** Minister of State for Emergency Management (sworn in November 20, 2026)
- **Riding:** Kamloops-North Thompson (directly adjacent to Cariboo-Chilcotin; elected 2024, re-elected 2026).
- **Prior Shadow Role:** Official Opposition Critic for Forests (Nov 2024–June 2026).

## Background & Career
- Former **Mayor of Barriere** (elected 2018, re-elected 2022) and member of Barriere's inaugural municipal council; career background in the Interior forest/logging sector.

## Cariboo-Chilcotin Impact
- Oversees Emergency Management BC (EMBC) alongside DM [[02_People/Lorie_Hrycuik]], directly governing wildfire evacuation protocols, risk-reduction mitigation (like the Watch Lake / 100 Mile burns), and flood/landslide disaster recovery in the Cariboo-Chilcotin.
