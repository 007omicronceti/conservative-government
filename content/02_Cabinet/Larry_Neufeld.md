# Larry Neufeld

## Role & Political Profile
- **Office:** Minister of Energy, Mines and Petroleum Resources (sworn in November 20, 2026)
- **Riding:** Peace River South (elected 2024; re-elected Oct 2026 with 77.40% / 6,243 votes).
- **Prior Shadow Role:** Official Opposition Critic for Natural Gas and LNG.
- **Junior Minister:** **Pete Davis** (Kootenay-Rockies), Minister of State for Mining.

## Background & Career
- Holds a Bachelor of Applied Science in Engineering (with graduate studies).
- Appointed VP of Western Canada's largest environmental remediation contractor at age 29; founded his own business in 2006; former Reserve Infantry Platoon Commander.

## Cariboo-Chilcotin Impact
- Oversees permitting and BC Hydro interconnection for the **Yellowhead Copper Project** (whose transmission line ties directly into the **100 Mile House BC Hydro substation**), plus **Cariboo Gold**, **Gibraltar**, and **Mount Polley**.
