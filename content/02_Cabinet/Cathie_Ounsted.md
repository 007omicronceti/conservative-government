# Cathie Ounsted

## Role & Political Profile
- **Office:** Minister of Local Government, Housing, Sport and Culture (sworn in November 20, 2026)
- **Junior Minister:** Zeeshan Wahla (Minister of State for Housing).

## Cariboo-Chilcotin Impact
- Primary provincial counterpart for the **District of 100 Mile House**, **City of Williams Lake**, and **Cariboo Regional District** on municipal infrastructure grants, zoning/housing mandates, and community sport/culture funding—paired with ex-Treasury Board DM [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Jonathan_Dube]].
