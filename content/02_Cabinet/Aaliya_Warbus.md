# Áʼa꞉líya Warbus

## Role & Political Profile
- **Office:** Minister of Environment (sworn in November 20, 2026)
- **Riding:** Chilliwack-Cultus Lake (re-elected MLA; Indigenous filmmaker, artist, and member of the Stó:lō Nation).
