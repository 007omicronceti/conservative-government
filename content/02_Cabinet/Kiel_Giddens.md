# Kiel Giddens

## Role & Political Profile
- **Office:** Minister of Forests (sworn in November 20, 2026)
- **Riding:** Prince George-Mackenzie (elected 2024; re-elected Oct 2026 with 65.81% / 10,473 votes).
- **Prior Shadow Role:** Official Opposition Critic for Labour (focused heavily on small business and forestry).

## Background & Career
- Born in Kamloops; former BC Liberal political staffer who worked on reducing environmental-assessment red tape and fast-tracking Interior infrastructure such as the **Cariboo Connector** and South Taylor Hill.
- Prior private-sector/industry-relations background (listed in historical BC ORL records prior to election as MLA).

## Cariboo-Chilcotin Impact
- Paired with Deputy Minister [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Janis_Simpkins]] (ex-Tolko VP), Giddens directly controls Cariboo timber supply, BC Timber Sales (BCTS), post-mill forestry recovery in 100 Mile House, and the Occupant License to Cut required for the **Yellowhead Copper Project**.
