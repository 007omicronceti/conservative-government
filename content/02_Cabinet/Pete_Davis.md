# Pete Davis

## Role & Political Profile
- **Office:** Minister of State for Mining (sworn in November 20, 2026)
- **Riding:** Kootenay-Rockies (re-elected Oct 2026).
- **Portfolio Home:** Reports within [[01_Ministries/08_Energy_Mines_and_Petroleum_Resources]] alongside Minister [[02_Cabinet/Larry_Neufeld]] and DM [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Nate_Amann-Blake]].

## Cariboo-Chilcotin Impact
- Dedicated point-minister for mining permits and critical minerals—directly overseeing the **Yellowhead Copper Project** (Taseko Mines), **Cariboo Gold Project**, **Gibraltar**, and **Mount Polley**.
