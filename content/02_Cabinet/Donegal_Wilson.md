# Donegal Wilson

## Role & Political Profile
- **Office:** Minister of Water, Land and Natural Resource Operations (sworn in November 20, 2026)
- **Riding:** Boundary-Similkameen (elected 2024; briefly joined Peter Milobar's CentreBC caucus in Sept 2026 before reuniting under Doerkson).
- **Prior Shadow Role:** Official Opposition Critic for Water, Land, Resource Stewardship and Wildlife Management.

## Background & Career
- Raised on a cattle ranch in the Otter Valley near Tulameen; lived 15 years in Smithers before returning to Keremeos (small engine business owner).
- Served 12 years (from 2012) as **Executive Director of the B.C. Snowmobile Federation**, sitting on ministerial committees and helping shape the *Off-Road Vehicle Act*.

## Cariboo-Chilcotin Impact
- Controls Crown land authorizations, range/grazing interface, water licensing, and backcountry recreation/wildlife policy across the Cariboo-Chilcotin—working with DM [[02_People/Kevin_Jardine]].
