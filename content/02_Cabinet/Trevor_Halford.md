# Trevor Halford

## Role & Political Profile
- **Office:** Minister of Transportation and Infrastructure (sworn in November 20, 2026)
- **Riding:** Surrey-White Rock (former BC Liberal/BC United MLA who crossed to BC Conservatives; former Official Opposition Critic for Transit and ICBC).

## Cariboo-Chilcotin Impact
- Controls the recombined Transportation and Infrastructure budget (working with DM [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Keith_Godin]]), directly responsible for Highway 97 maintenance, passing lanes, commercial truck safety north/south of 100 Mile House and Quesnel, and provincial capital project delivery.
