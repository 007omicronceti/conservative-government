# Korky Neufeld

## Role & Political Profile
- **Office:** Minister of Post-Secondary Education and Labour (sworn in November 20, 2026)
- **Riding:** Abbotsford West (second-term MLA).
- **Junior Minister:** Harman Bhangu (Langley-Abbotsford, second-term MLA) as Minister of State for Labour.

## Cariboo-Chilcotin Impact
- Oversees labour relations (including the active **Cariboo Gold Project** camp-worker strike mandate), WorkSafeBC rates for forestry/mining employers, and regional trades/post-secondary funding (TRU Williams Lake) alongside DM [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Trevor_Hughes]].
