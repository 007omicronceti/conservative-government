# Peter Milobar — Minister of Finance

**Peter Milobar** is the MLA for **Kamloops Centre** and was sworn in as British Columbia's **Minister of Finance** on November 20, 2026, in Premier [[02_Cabinet/Lorne_Doerkson]]'s first cabinet.

## Why He Matters
As Finance Minister, Milobar controls the provincial purse strings, tax policy, and capital spending approvals. Because there is no fall legislative sitting, his first major test will be the **Spring 2027 Budget**, which will determine how the new government funds rural healthcare, highways, and resource infrastructure while delivering promised tax cuts.

## Background & Track Record
- **Municipal Leadership:** Former Mayor of Kamloops, giving him deep familiarity with Interior municipal budgets, property tax pressures, and infrastructure funding gaps.
- **Legislative Experience:** Incumbent MLA who served as the Opposition Finance Critic prior to the 2026 election, making him one of the most experienced fiscal hands in cabinet.
- **Ministry Link:** [[01_Ministries/10_Finance]]

## Key Policy Files to Watch
- **Tax Relief & Reform:** Implementing the Conservative platform's promised provincial income tax cuts, a three-year PST exemption on productive business capital (machinery, equipment, software), and a 180-day blue-ribbon tax reform panel.
- **Capital Spending Review:** Auditing and reviewing provincial capital projects for cost discipline and transparency.
- **Fiscal Discipline:** Balancing deficit reduction against rural service demands.

## Cariboo–Chilcotin Corridor Impact
- **Healthcare Capital:** Funding and construction timelines for the Cariboo Memorial Hospital redevelopment in Williams Lake and ER staffing stability in 100 Mile House.
- **Highway 97 & Resource Infrastructure:** Capital allocation for Highway 97 upgrades, transmission lines, and permitting capacity for regional mining and forestry projects.
- **Local Government Funding:** Provincial grants and revenue-sharing affecting the Cariboo Regional District, Williams Lake, and 100 Mile House.
