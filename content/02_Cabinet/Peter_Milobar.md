# Peter Milobar

## Role & Political Profile
- **Office:** Minister of Finance (sworn in November 20, 2026)
- **Riding:** Kamloops Centre (former Mayor of Kamloops; veteran BC Liberal/BC United/Conservative MLA; briefly led CentreBC after leaving Kerry-Lynne Findlay's caucus before cutting a deal to rejoin under Doerkson).

## Cariboo-Chilcotin Impact
- As Interior-based Finance Minister paired with returning Finance DM [[02_People/Heather_Wood]], Milobar controls Treasury Board approvals for every Highway 97 upgrade, rural healthcare funding envelope, and post-mill economic diversification grant in the Cariboo-Chilcotin.
