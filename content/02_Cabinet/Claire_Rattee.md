# Claire Rattée
**17th Deputy Premier of British Columbia | Minister of Social Development | MLA for Skeena**

* **Office:** [[01_Ministries/17_Social_Development|Ministry of Social Development]] & Deputy Premier (sworn in November 20, 2026)
* **Deputy Minister:** [[03_Deputy_Ministers/Tara_Richards|Tara Richards]]
* **Electoral District:** Skeena (Terrace, Kitimat, and surrounding northwestern communities)

## 1. Early Life & Pre-Political Career
Born in Delta, B.C. in 1991, Claire Rattée moved to Kitimat in 2011, where she established and operated a local small business before entering municipal politics. She later served four years on Kitimat City Council—becoming the youngest woman ever elected to council in Kitimat—focusing on local economic development, healthcare access, and affordable housing.

## 2. Electoral & Caucus Record (2019–2026)
* **Federal Campaigns (2019 & 2021):** Ran twice as the Conservative Party of Canada candidate in Skeena—Bulkley Valley against NDP MP Taylor Bachrach, increasing the Conservative vote share from 24% to 36%.
* **2024 Provincial Election:** Nominated on January 30, 2024 and won Skeena with 51.20% of the vote, succeeding outgoing BC United MLA Ellis Ross and becoming the first woman elected MLA for Skeena.
* **Opposition Portfolio:** Served in the Official Opposition shadow cabinet as Critic for Mental Health and Addictions, advocating for low-income housing, emergency weather shelters, and treatment capacity.
* **September 2026 Caucus Expulsion & Return:** On September 18, 2026, Rattée, [[02_Cabinet/Trevor_Halford|Trevor Halford]], and former leader John Rustad were removed from caucus by Kerry-Lynne Findlay; all three were reinstated three days later upon Findlay's resignation. Following the October 24, 2026 election, Premier [[02_Cabinet/Lorne_Doerkson|Lorne Doerkson]] appointed her Deputy Premier and Minister of Social Development.

## 3. Cariboo-Chilcotin & 100 Mile House Accountability Watchlist
* **Rural Shelters, Income Assistance & Addictions Support:** Oversees income/disability assistance rates, community social service contracts, and extreme-weather shelter funding affecting Williams Lake and 100 Mile House.
* **Northern-Interior Cabinet Balance:** As Deputy Premier from northern B.C. (Skeena), pairs with Cariboo-Chilcotin Premier [[02_Cabinet/Lorne_Doerkson|Lorne Doerkson]] in setting regional rural priorities across northern and Interior communities.
