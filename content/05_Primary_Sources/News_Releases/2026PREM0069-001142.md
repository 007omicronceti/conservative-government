New cabinet sworn in with focus on restoring confidence, building a stronger B.C.

VICTORIA – Premier Lorne Doerkson has unveiled British Columbia’s new cabinet, bringing together a team focused on restoring confidence in government and delivering a stronger future for people throughout the province.

“British Columbians voted for change and our government is ready to deliver it,” said Premier Doerkson. “Our goal is to restore the belief that British Columbia is the best place in the world, and we do that by making life more affordable, supercharging our economy, helping our most vulnerable citizens and protecting the rights and freedoms of all British Columbians.”

The new cabinet will focus on five priorities:

* An Affordable B.C. by delivering tax relief, reducing costs, making housing more attainable and restoring fiscal discipline.

* A Prosperous B.C. by restoring investment confidence, responsibly developing B.C.’s resources, strengthening industries and trade and creating good jobs.

* A Healthy B.C. by protecting universal health care, while improving access, treatment, recovery and support for those who need it.

* A Safer B.C. by strengthening policing and justice, supporting victims and preparing communities for wildfires, floods and other emergencies.

* A Strong and Proud B.C. by strengthening education and families, protecting freedoms and B.C.’s  environment, advancing reconciliation and economic partnerships with First Nations and standing up for British Columbia.
