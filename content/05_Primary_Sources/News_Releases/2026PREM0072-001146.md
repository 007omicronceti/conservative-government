Premier Doerkson sets priorities for new government in mandate letters

VICTORIA – Premier Lorne Doerkson has issued mandate letters to cabinet ministers and ministers of state, outlining the priorities, expectations and responsibilities that will guide the new government's work to deliver meaningful change for British Columbians.

British Columbians have given the government a clear mandate for change. After nearly a decade, costs have climbed for families and the province is running record deficits. Investment has been leaving for other jurisdictions, people wait too long for health care, housing is out of reach for many young people and communities feel less safe. The letters tell ministers that they hold a mandate to fix these problems.

The government's plan rests on five goals: An Affordable B.C., A Prosperous B.C., A Healthy B.C., A Safer B.C. and A Strong and Proud B.C. Each minister will report on their priorities against those goals, and each is expected to commit to a measurable result and a delivery date.

Fiscal discipline is the job of every minister, not only the Minister of Finance. New spending needs clear priorities and demonstrable outcomes, and any new funding request goes to Treasury Board, where affordability for taxpayers is a test. Commitments that cross ministries are to be worked out jointly by the ministers involved.

“British Columbians voted for change, and our government is getting to work delivering it,” said Premier Doerkson. “These mandate letters make clear what British Columbians expect each minister to accomplish. They are about results and accountability, and ensuring our government delivers on the priorities that matter most to British Columbians.”
