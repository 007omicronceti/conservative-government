# Mandate Letter — Kiel Giddens (November 24, 2026)

Honourable Kiel Giddens
Minister of Forests
Parliament Buildings
Victoria, BC V8V 1X4

Dear Minister:

British Columbians voted overwhelmingly for change. Families keep facing higher costs,
the Province’s deficits are at record levels, and investment has been leaving British
Columbia for other jurisdictions when it should be staying here. People are waiting too
long for the health care they need. Housing is out of reach for too many, especially young
people. Our communities feel less safe. And we’ve lost sight of the basics in our schools
and our democratic institutions.

We have been given a mandate to fix these problems, and as a Minister it is your
responsibility to help deliver the change that British Columbians voted for. This is our
moment to meet the challenge, make the hard decisions, and deliver the change British
Columbians expect.

Our Priorities

Our plan is built on delivering on five goals. Each direction in this letter advances one or
more of them, and I expect you to plan and report your priorities in these terms.

An Affordable BC: We will make British Columbians’ hard work pay off, deliver tax relief,
reduce costs, make housing more attainable, and restore fiscal discipline.

A Prosperous BC: We will supercharge our economy, restore investor confidence, develop
our resources responsibly for the benefit of British Columbians, strengthen industries and
trade, and create more good jobs.

A Healthy BC: We will protect universal health care, and improve access, treatment,
recovery and support for those who need it, when they need it.

A Safer BC: We will restore confidence in our communities, strengthen policing and
justice, support victims, and prepare communities for wildfires, floods and other
emergencies.

A Strong and Proud BC: We will build pride in our province and confidence in our shared
future, strengthen education and families, protect freedoms and our environment,
advance reconciliation and economic partnerships with First Nations, and stand up for
British Columbia.

Together, these goals will guide the choices we make and the results we deliver. I expect
you to set out the expected outcomes and benefits that British Columbians should see
from our actions, when we will deliver, and how we will measure results.

How We Will Succeed

We will work with the professional public service, First Nations, local governments, businesses
and communities to move from plans to implementation with urgency and purpose.

Note that restoring fiscal discipline is a central obligation of every minister.

We must confront the structural pressures in the budget, scrutinize programs and
spending, eliminate duplication, measure value for money and make difficult choices
where necessary. New spending must be justified by clear priorities and demonstrable
outcomes. Every minister is accountable for helping return the Province to sustainable
finances while protecting essential services and creating the conditions for growth.

Any new funding requests will be subject to thorough and rigorous review by the Treasury
Board, with affordability for provincial taxpayers as a test, and must be delivered within
the government’s Fiscal Plan.

Many of our commitments need the work of more than one ministry. Where actions or
authority to deliver on our mandate overlaps among ministries, you will work
collaboratively with your ministerial colleagues to seek the best outcome.

Parliamentary Secretaries will support Ministers in their portfolio. Their roles and
responsibilities will be set out by the Minister, consistent with the directions in this letter.

Your Priorities and Delivery Expectations

As Minister, you will play a central role in advancing our government's priorities and
delivering the commitments we have made to British Columbians.

In addition to your statutory responsibilities, I expect you to focus on the following
assignments:

Working with your Deputy Minister and Cabinet colleagues, you will establish clear plans,
timelines, expected results, and measures of success, with a focus on early and meaningful
progress. I expect you to report regularly on the progress you are making to deliver our
priorities, the results achieved and the challenges that remain.

Our Responsibility

Public office carries a fundamental responsibility: to serve all British Columbians, whether
or not they supported us, and to act in their best interests at all times. The public has
placed its trust in us at a difficult time, and we must earn that trust every day through
principled decisions, fiscal discipline and meaningful results.

Every minister has a responsibility to control spending, eliminate waste, exercise restraint
and direct public resources where they deliver the greatest value. We must also challenge
unnecessary regulation and honour our commitment to eliminate two existing regulatory
provisions for every new one we introduce.

The challenges before us are significant, and the decisions ahead will not always be easy.
Restoring fiscal responsibility will require difficult choices, the courage to confront
longstanding problems and the discipline to put the province's long-term interests ahead
of short-term considerations. We cannot continue to spend beyond our means or leave
the consequences of today's decisions to future generations.

I look forward to working with you to deliver the commitments we made to British Columbians.

Sincerely,




Honourable Lorne Doerkson
Premier of British Columbia
