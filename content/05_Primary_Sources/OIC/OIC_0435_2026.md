PROVINCE OF BRITISH COLUMBIA

                    ORDER OF THE LIEUTENANT GOVERNOR IN COUNCIL

Order in Council No.     435                          , Approved and Ordered                    November 21, 2026



                                                                                                 Lieutenant Governor

Executive Council Chambers, Victoria

       On the recommendation of the undersigned, the Lieutenant Governor, by and with the advice and consent of the
Executive Council, orders that, effective November 21, 2026,
           (a) the appointment of Jenelyn Agias Hontiveros as an Executive Coordinator in the Office of the Premier, made
               by Order in Council 37/2025, is rescinded,
           (b) the appointment of Molly Laura Wilkins as an Executive Coordinator in the Office of the Premier, made by
               Order in Council 138/2026, is rescinded, and
           (c) paragraph (b) of Order in Council 138/2026 is repealed.




                                                                                 Presiding Member of the Executive Council



                                       (This part is for administrative purposes only and is not part of the Order.)

Authority under which Order is made:

Act and section:   Public Service Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 385, s. 15
