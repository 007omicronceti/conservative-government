               SAFE ACCESS TO SCHOOLS REGULATION
Definition
    1         In this regulation, “Act” means the Safe Access to Schools Act.

Access zones
    2         Access zones are established for the following schools:
                   (a) schools operated by a board of education, other than the Qwam Qwum
                       Stuwixwulh Community School in the City of Nanaimo;
                   (b) schools operated by a francophone education authority;
                   (c) schools operated by an independent school authority, other than the schools
                       set out in column 2 of the Schedule to this regulation.

Designated policing unit
    3         For the purposes of the definition of “police officer” in section 1 of the Act, the
              designated policing unit known as the Stl’atl’imx Tribal Police Service is prescribed.
