                opposite the person's name in Column 3.

                A person named in Column 1 of the attached Schedule 2 is appointed to the position

                opposite the person's name in Column 3.

                A person appointed to a position under section 1 is to be paid in accordance with the
                band 3 classification set out in the British Columbia Management Classification and
                Compensation Framework administered by the Public Service Agency.

                A person appointed to a position under section 2 is to be paid in accordance with the
                band 2 classification set out in the British Columbia Management Classification and
                Compensation Framework administered by the Public Service Agency.

                Terms and conditions of employment equivalent to those established for category C
                appointees in the "Terms and Conditions for Excluded Employees and Appointees"
                administered by the Public Service Agency apply to a person appointed to a position
                under section 1 or 2.

                If a person appointed to a position under section 1 or 2 of this order held a position by
                appointment under section 15 of the Public Service Act immediately before the making
                of the appointment under section 1 or 2, that earlier appointment is rescinded.
