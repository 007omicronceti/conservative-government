1         The Designation and Exemption Regulation, B.C. Reg. 168/90, is amended by adding
          the following sections:

Definition
    0.1      In this regulation, “migratory game bird” means a migratory game bird as defined
             in the Migratory Birds Convention Act, 1994 (Canada).

Exemption from section 27 (1) of the Wildlife Act
for actions taken from boat – wildlife other than migratory game birds
    5.1      A person who discharges a firearm or wounds or kills wildlife, other than migratory
             game birds, from a boat is exempt from section 27 (1) of the Wildlife Act, in relation
             to that action, if
                    (a) the action is taken
                            (i) while the boat is not being propelled by the motor,
                           (ii) while the boat is held stationary at a fixed position in current, tide or
                                wind by the power of the motor, or
                          (iii) while the boat is propelled by the motor in tidal waters at a low speed
                                so that the boat is not planing and is not moving faster than necessary,
                                as a result of the waves or wind, to establish a level and stable
                                platform for the purpose of taking the action, and
                   (b) the action is taken for the purposes of
                            (i) hunting or trapping wildlife other than migratory game birds, or
                           (ii) retrieving wounded, injured or dead wildlife other than migratory
                                game birds.

Exemption from section 27 (1) of the Wildlife Act
for actions taken from boat – migratory game birds
    5.2      A person who discharges a firearm or wounds or kills migratory game birds from a
             boat is exempt from section 27 (1) of the Wildlife Act, in relation to that action, if
                   (a) the action is taken while the boat is not being propelled by the motor, and
                   (b) the action is taken for the purposes of
                          (i) hunting migratory game birds, or
                         (ii) retrieving wounded, injured or dead migratory game birds.

2         Section 14 (b) is amended by striking out “as defined in the Migratory Birds Convention
          Act, 1994 (Canada)”.

3         The following sections are added:

Definitions for sections 19 to 22

           “firearm” means a firearm as defined in section 1 of the Firearm Violence
               Prevention Act;
           “loaded” has the same meaning as in section 4 of the Firearm Violence Prevention
               Act.

Exemption from section 4 (1) of Firearm Violence Prevention Act
for hunting or trapping from boat – wildlife other than migratory game birds
  19      (1) A person who carries a loaded firearm in or on a boat is exempt from section 4 (1)
              of the Firearm Violence Prevention Act, in relation to that action, if
               (a) the person carries the loaded firearm
                       (i) in or on a boat that does not have a motor, or
                      (ii) in the case of a boat that has a motor,
                            (A) while the boat is not being propelled by the motor,
                            (B) subject to subsection (2) of this section, while the boat is held
                                 stationary at a fixed position in current, tide or wind by the
                                 power of the motor, or
                            (C) subject to subsection (2) of this section, while the boat is
                                 propelled by the motor in tidal waters at a low speed so that the
                                 boat is not planing and is not moving faster than necessary, as a
                                 result of the waves or wind, to establish a level and stable
                                 platform for the purpose of discharging the firearm, and
               (b) the action is taken for the purposes of
                       (i) hunting or trapping wildlife other than migratory game birds, or
                      (ii) retrieving wounded, injured or dead wildlife other than migratory
                           game birds.
          (2) In the circumstances set out in subsection (1) (a) (ii) (B) or (C), the exemption
              only applies immediately before, during and immediately after the discharge of
              the firearm in accordance with section 21.

Exemption from section 4 (1) of Firearm Violence Prevention Act
for hunting from boat – migratory game birds
  20     A person who carries a loaded firearm in or on a boat is exempt from section 4 (1) of
         the Firearm Violence Prevention Act, in relation to that action, if
               (a) the person carries the loaded firearm
                      (i) in or on a boat that does not have a motor and is not being propelled
                          by a sail, or
                     (ii) in the case of a boat that has a motor, while the boat is not being
                          propelled by the motor or a sail, and
               (b) the action is taken for the purposes of
                      (i) hunting migratory game birds, or
                     (ii) retrieving wounded, injured or dead migratory game birds.
