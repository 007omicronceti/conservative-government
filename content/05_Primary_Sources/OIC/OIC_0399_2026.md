Transfer of regional district service jurisdiction
    5      The jurisdiction for a service of the regional district that is provided in a service area, all or part of which service
           area is in the transferred area, is transferred to the municipality in respect of all or that part of the service area that
           is in the transferred area.

Transfer of regional district’s assets
    6      The regional district’s rights, property and assets that relate to a service transferred under section 5 and that are
           associated with all or part of the transferred area are transferred to and vested in the municipality.

Transfer of regional district’s obligations
    7      The regional district’s obligations that relate to a service transferred under section 5 and that are associated with
           all or part of the transferred area are transferred to and assumed by the municipality.

Regional district resolution continued
    8      A resolution of the regional district that relates to a service for which jurisdiction is transferred under section 5
           and that is applicable to all or part of the transferred area continues in force as a resolution of the municipality
           applicable to the area of the municipality to which it applied as a resolution of the regional district until the
           resolution is amended or repealed by the council of the municipality.

Regional district reference deemed a municipal reference
    9      A reference to the regional district in any commercial paper, lease, license, permit or other contract, instrument
           or document that is transferred under section 6 or 7 or that relates to rights, property, assets and obligations
           transferred under section 6 or 7 is deemed to be a reference to the municipality.
