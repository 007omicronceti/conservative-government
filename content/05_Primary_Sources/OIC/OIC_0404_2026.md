        KITSUMKALUM INTERIM PROTECTION AREA ORDER
Definitions
    1     (1) In this order:
              “electric transmission line project” has the same meaning as in the Renewable
                  Energy Projects (Streamlined Permitting) Act;
              “interim protection area” means the Kitsumkalum Interim Protection Area
                  established by section 2;
              “interim protection period” means the period beginning on the date this order is
                  made and ending on September 25, 2028;
              “issuing official” means a person referred to in section 170 (2) (b) of the Forest Act;
              “North Coast Transmission Line project” has the same meaning as in the
                 Renewable Energy Projects (Streamlined Permitting) Act;
              “regulator” has the same meaning as in the Energy Resource Activities Act;
              “specified authorization” means a permit, licence or plan referred to in
                 section 170 (3) (a), (b), (c), (g), (h), (i) or (j) of the Forest Act.
          (2) For certainty, the regulator is not an issuing official for the purposes of this order.

Kitsumkalum Interim Protection Area
    2     The Kitsumkalum Interim Protection Area is established, consisting of the land
          described in Schedule A.

Forest Act – specified authorizations
    3     (1) Subject to subsection (3), an issuing official may not, during the interim
              protection period, issue a specified authorization that applies to any part of the
              interim protection area.
          (2) Subject to subsections (3) and (4), the rights under a cutting permit, road permit
              or timber sale licence under the Forest Act that applies to any part of the interim
              protection area are suspended in relation to that part.
          (3) Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply in relation to any of the following:
               (a) a road permit listed in column 1 of Schedule B as the permit relates to a
                   section of road set out opposite in column 2;
               (b) a specified authorization that relates to the North Coast Transmission Line
                   project or another electric transmission line project.
          (4) Subsection (2) does not apply in relation to a cutting permit that is issued in
              accordance with section 8 [regulator’s responsibilities under specified
              enactments] of the Energy Resource Activities Act.

Forest Act – allowable annual cut
    4     Sections 173 to 175 of the Forest Act apply in relation to the interim protection area
          as though that area were a designated area under section 169 of that Act.
