PROVINCE OF BRITISH COLUMBIA

                    ORDER OF THE LIEUTENANT GOVERNOR IN COUNCIL

Order in Council No.   390                            , Approved and Ordered                 September 2, 2026



                                                                                                  Lieutenant Governor

Executive Council Chambers, Victoria

       On the recommendation of the undersigned, the Lieutenant Governor, by and with the advice and consent of the
Executive Council, orders that
           (a) the appointment of Seina Murase Cho as a Public Affairs Officer, Government Communications and Public
               Engagement, Ministry of Finance, made by Order in Council 464/2024, is rescinded,
           (b) Seina Murase Cho is appointed as a Senior Public Affairs Officer, Government Communications and Public
               Engagement, Ministry of Finance,
           (c) Seina Murase Cho is to be paid in accordance with the band 2 classification set out in the British Columbia
               Management Classification and Compensation Framework administered by the Public Service Agency,
           (d) terms and conditions of employment equivalent to those established for category A appointees in the “Terms
               and Conditions for Excluded Employees and Appointees” administered by the Public Service Agency apply
               to the appointment of Seina Murase Cho, and
           (e) effective September 14, 2026,
                 (i) the appointment of Yee Ha Cheng as Manager, Multi‐language Services, Government Communications
                     and Public Engagement, Ministry of Finance, made by Order in Council 110/2023, is rescinded,
                (ii) Yee Ha Cheng is appointed as Director, Multi‐language Services, Government Communications and
                     Public Engagement, Ministry of Finance,
               (iii) Yee Ha Cheng is to be paid in accordance with the band 4 classification set out in the British Columbia
                     Management Classification and Compensation Framework administered by the Public Service Agency,
                     and
               (iv) terms and conditions of employment equivalent to those established for category A appointees in the
                     “Terms and Conditions for Excluded Employees and Appointees” administered by the Public Service
                     Agency apply to the appointment of Yee Ha Cheng.




                                                                                 Presiding Member of the Executive Council



                                       (This part is for administrative purposes only and is not part of the Order.)

Authority under which Order is made:

Act and section:   Public Service Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 385, s. 15
        Other:     OIC 110/2023; OIC 464/2024
